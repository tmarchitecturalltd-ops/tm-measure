### Description (4000)
```
TM Measure turns your phone into a professional room-measuring tool, so you can send accurate dimensions to your architect without waiting for — or paying for — a site visit.

Built by TM Architectural Designs Ltd. for homeowners planning an extension, loft conversion, or refurbishment, TM Measure walks you through every room in a single guided session. Tap four corners, snap your reference photos, drag your rooms into a quick floor plan, and submit the whole thing in about the time it takes to make a cup of tea.

KEY FEATURES

• Guided project wizard — name the job, lock your units (metric or imperial), then work through your rooms one at a time.
• Calibrated camera capture — a quick scale-bar step solves your phone's true focal length so wall lengths land within ±3 cm. We tap each corner three times and use the median to absorb finger tremor.
• LiDAR on iPhone Pro and iPad Pro — when your device supports it, we use Apple's RoomPlan for centimetre-grade accuracy with no calibration needed.
• Live accuracy pill — High / Medium / Low updates in real time as you point the camera, so you know what to expect before you tap.
• Mandatory reference photos — every room carries at least one photo so the architect can audit the dimensions visually against what's actually there: radiators, columns, chimney breasts, etc.
• Drag-and-drop floor plan — arrange your rooms on a simple grid so the architect understands how they connect, including multi-storey buildings.
• Anomaly checks before submission — ceiling-too-low, wall-too-long, and missing-photo issues are caught before the form leaves your phone.
• Metric and imperial — pick once, see both side-by-side on the review screen.
• Works offline — the wizard runs without a network connection. You only need to be online to submit.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your camera frames are processed on-device — only the final numeric dimensions and the compressed reference photos you choose to send leave your phone. We don't use third-party analytics, we don't collect your location, and we don't share your data with advertisers.

ABOUT TM ARCHITECTURAL DESIGNS LTD.

We are a UK architectural practice specialising in residential extensions, loft conversions, and home refurbishments. TM Measure is our answer to a question we got tired of answering on the phone: "can you come and measure up before you quote?" Now you can do it yourself, send us the numbers, and we can get straight to the design work — usually saving £200–£300 in site-visit fees.

GET STARTED

Download the app, start a project, and step through your rooms. When you are done, tap Submit and your project details arrive in our inbox. We reply with a design quote, usually within two working days.

Questions? Email us at inquiries@tmdesignsltd.com.
```

## Sanity-check before you submit

1. **Replace the three URL placeholders** above with the real URLs once they're live.
2. **Read the description out loud.** If any sentence makes you wince, change it — you know the business voice better than I do.
3. **Claim counts.** The description says auto-scan works on "supported devices" (Android) and specifically calls out LiDAR on "iPhone Pro and iPad Pro" (iOS). Don't upgrade those to "all devices" unless you've tested and know it's true — Apple rejection risk.
4. **"£200–£300 site-visit fees."** If your actual site-visit fee is different, change it. Factual claims in store copy have to match reality.
